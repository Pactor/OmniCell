Native client proof: MessageProtocol.dll Message_t::CreateDataBlock creates bytes 2–15; Connection.dll Connection_t::Send at RVA 0x16F3 writes bytes 0–1, 4–7; N3.dll n3InfoItemRemote_t::Write at RVA 0x98F4 writes opcode, affected identity, and ToBePassedOn at bytes 16–28. Retail captures independently match those writes. Packet body: How much of each district's suppression field is up. Every field of it was an Unknown, and the client names all of them. Extracted-client FightModeUpdate_t - no IIR suffix, which is why the older name scan missed it - has vtable 0x1017101C, reader 0x10125055, writer 0x10124E9E and dispatcher 0x101250C3, and the client calls the list entries FightModeChange_t in its own words: "Invalid FightModeChange_t stream" is what the entry reader prints when it refuses one. The message Identity is a playfield: the dispatcher hands its instance half to the playfield lookup and then resolves every district name against that playfield's district table, through GameData's PlayfieldDistrictInfo_t::GetDistrictData - the overload that takes a string, which is what settles the name as a name. The int in front of each name is the change's own id, and the reason it exists is the third flag bit: a change carrying it is matched against the district's existing changes on that id, at 0x1011FE65, rather than being added by name. The other two bits are what the change does. 0x1011FDDD is the routine that works a district's level out - it walks the district's changes, assigns on the first bit and adds without it, skips every change after an override that is not itself an override, and clamps the total to 0 through 4. The last byte is that level, and the client spells the five values: 0x1003F4A3 switches on it and loads SuppressionField100, SuppressionField75, SuppressionField25, SuppressionField5 or SuppressionField0. Zero is the most suppression and four is none. The reader enforces the range from both sides - 0 to 4 on a change that sets, -4 to 4 on one that adds, which is why the byte is read signed. All nine captured copies are one Notum Wars playfield setting its nine districts to seventy five percent.
