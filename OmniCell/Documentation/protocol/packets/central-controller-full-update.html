The reader refuses a first byte above 3 at 0x1009F492 and a second above 2 at 0x1009F499, and the dispatcher at 0x1009F441 hands the first to 0x1007E1F1 and the second to 0x1007E206 - the same setter CentralControllerState's single byte goes to. 0x1007E165 switches on the first to pick mine, sentry or fence, and 0x1007E14A switches on the second to pick Active, Inactive or Destroyed, and the client assembles the controller's display name from the two with the format "%s %s controller" at 0x10162F10. The captured copies carry kind 2 and state 0, and the Name field twenty five bytes earlier reads "Active sentry controller", so the bytes and the name agree without anything being assumed. 0x1007E258 confirms the state independently: it plays ControllerDeactivated when the value is 1 and ControllerDestroyed otherwise, which is why zero plays nothing. Three copies appear in one captured mission run and none in any earlier capture; before this the message had no C# at all and was one of five in that state.
