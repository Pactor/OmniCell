Both branches bump the manager's revision counter at +0x20 and both re-run the marker geometry the field draws over its towers, which is what 0x1012A052 and 0x1012A029 are. So the int32 is an add or a remove: 1 takes the tower named by the message's own Identity out, 2 puts the one carried after it in.
