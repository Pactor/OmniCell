It lives in GUI.dll at 0x100F7C92 surrounded by Allocate Research, Available Research, Finished Research, esc_research and the personal and global scroll lists - that is the research window - and it is one of a trio with got_ip and got_perk. Two messages, one table. Their signals sit next to each other too: this one emits on GlobalSignals_c + 0x240 and ResearchUpdate on + 0x244.
