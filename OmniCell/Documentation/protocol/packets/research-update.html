The reader is short and every instruction in it is accounted for, which is what lets the leading byte be called discarded rather than merely unobserved. Another of the classes the old IIR_t-only scan could not see - ResearchUpdateIIR, with no suffix at all - vtable 0x10157078, reader 0x1003A7A0. It fetches the research table singleton from 0x1002C0D5, reads one byte into a stack slot and never touches that slot again, and then loops: an int32 id, and it stops when the id is zero, so the list has no count in front of it. For a non-zero id it reads three int32s in a loop with the index as the counter, handing each to 0x1002C063 with that index. A failed lookup reads all three and drops them.

What kept this amber was the three. 0x1002C063 refuses an index above 2 and otherwise picks one of three lists on the table - at + 4, + 0x14 and + 0x24 - copies the seven word research definition into a new thirty two byte slot and writes the value into the eighth word. So the three values are the same kind of thing three times over, filed under three headings. The headings were the question. The client carries four research stats - 263 personalresearchlevel, 264 globalresearchlevel, 265 personalresearchgoal and 266 globalresearchgoal - and four does not divide into three; and all three values are zero in every one of the 54 entries of all 17 captured copies, which is what a character who has researched nothing looks like, so the wire could not separate them either.

The client separates them. The accessor at 0x10027820 takes a research id, looks it up in the same table through 0x1002BD8C, and branches on a flags word at definition + 0xC: bit 7 is global research and bit 6 is personal. The global branch reads stat 0x21 - 33, side - off the character at [dynel + 0xE8] vtable + 0x3C and passes it straight into 0x1002BE1A as an index. 0x1002BE1A caps that index at 2 and computes [table + index * 16 + 4], which is the same three lists 0x1002C063 writes into, in the same order. Side is 0 Neutral, 1 Clan, 2 Omni, and there are exactly three of them. That is what the three values are: one per side, not one per heading. Four stats never had to divide into three.

What the value is comes from the slot. 0x1002C063 puts it in the eighth word, + 0x1C of the thirty two byte slot, and 0x1002BE50 tests that same + 0x1C for being negative to decide whether the entry has been filled in yet. PerkUpdate writes experience still owed to + 0x1C of a slot with the same layout, on the character's own personal map at dynel + 0x1F4, and computes its progress bar against it. Same offset, same field, and the two messages are the global and personal halves of one system - their signals are neighbours as well, GlobalSignals_c + 0x244 here and + 0x240 there.

The personal branch is worth stating so that nobody looks for it here: bit 6 definitions are not carried by this message at all. It fills the three global side lists and nothing else.
